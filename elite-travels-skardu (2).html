Where we take you

A short list of a very large region.

Every route below is one we run ourselves — ask about anywhere else in Gilgit‑Baltistan and we’ll likely still get you there.

2,500M · SKARDU

Shangrila Lake

The lake everyone photographs first, and the easiest overnight from town.

4,114M · PLATEAU

Deosai Plains

Open highland grazing land at four thousand metres — brown bears, wide sky, no trees.

2,560M · VALLEY

Khaplu Valley

Apricot orchards, a 19th-century fort, and the quiet approach road to the Karakoram.

2,438M · NORTH

Hunza Valley

Terraced fields against Rakaposhi — the long way round, and worth every hour.

5,150M · BASE CAMP

K2 Base Camp Trek

Our longest route — a multi-day trek toward the world’s second-highest peak.

The idea is a plain one: travellers coming into Gilgit‑Baltistan should be able to reach someone who actually knows the region, before they arrive and while they’re here.
